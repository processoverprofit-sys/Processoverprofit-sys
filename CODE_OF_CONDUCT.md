# Community code of conduct

This applies to public interactions in this repository. Participation is voluntary and subject to the maintainer's review.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, constructive and specific. Challenge engineering ideas and evidence, not individuals.
- Distinguish observations from assumptions; disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy, authorship and the documented scope of contribution.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, impersonation, spam, scams, requests for secrets or funds, and disclosure of private or sensitive material are not acceptable. Do not seek access to or test non-public systems without explicit authorisation.

## Moderation and reporting

The maintainer may decline contributions, remove inappropriate content, limit interaction or use GitHub's reporting tools as appropriate. Report platform abuse through GitHub's reporting controls. Security concerns belong in the confidential route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), if available; do not post sensitive details publicly. No response-time commitment is made.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for contribution scope. This document does not change repository or Discord permissions, establish a contractual relationship or grant private access.
