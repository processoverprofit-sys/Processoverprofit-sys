# Frequently asked questions

## What is ProcessOverProfit?

An independent, documentation-focused engineering portfolio for two private, local-first software projects. [Engineering HQ](../README.md) connects their public documentation and development roadmap.

## Can I download T-Bot or BrainBot?

No public application release is available. The repositories contain public descriptions, conceptual designs and illustrative examples rather than the private applications. Public visibility does not grant a licence to the private software.

## Is T-Bot operating or accepting funds?

No qualified operational trading system or live-money authorisation is claimed. No one is being asked to deposit funds, connect a wallet or rely on a return. See [T-Bot development status](https://github.com/processoverprofit-sys/T-Bot/blob/main/docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md).

## Is BrainBot complete?

Its PC 3.5.0–3.5.3 historical development line is recorded as completed and frozen. This does not establish a public release, general production readiness or independent certification. See [BrainBot development status](https://github.com/processoverprofit-sys/BrainBot/blob/main/docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md).

## Who reviews the work?

Development is owner-led and AI-assisted. AI assistance is not independent human assurance. No completed independent professional audit is claimed in these public documents.

## Are the test examples actual results?

No. Public examples are fictional and unexecuted. The [evidence report template](EVIDENCE_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md) describes how future reviewed results could be presented; it contains no actual evidence.

## How can I contribute or join Discord?

Read the [community guide](COMMUNITY.md) and [contribution policy](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Public Issues and Discussions are disabled. A direct Discord application link has not yet been verified for publication; the guide explains the current invitation-request route.

## How do I report a security concern?

Read [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Use a confirmed confidential route only, never a public comment or pull request. Do not independently test private infrastructure.
